Writing Exercise 2

Monday, April 13th

Question: What is a human-made creation that brings out the best in you?

Music brings out the best in me. There are so many songs out there that capture every human thought and feeling imaginable, and I can always find ones I relate to. When I listen to music, it can calm me down and help me focus, or it can make me feel excited, sad, frustrated, or any emotion in between. Not only that, music is something you can create. I grew up playing the piano and the violin, and those hours of practice over the years taught me to be disciplined and consistent. Even when I didn’t want to get my instrument out and when certain passages felt impossible, I had to try again and think about how I could improve. It also gave me an appreciation for the arts and an understanding that good things come from patience and putting your effort into the right things.
