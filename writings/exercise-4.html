Writing Exercise 4

Monday, April 20th

Question: What is something you appreciate or long for from the past? Why?

Something I appreciate from the past is my parents' home-cooked meals and our family dinners, which we'd make time for at least a few times a week when our schedules aligned. It was a forced pause in my evenings, and regardless of how stressed we were or how much work we had to do, we all agreed to put everything down for half an hour and be fully present with one another at the dinner table. Now, in college, food is often treated transactionally, and I often eat while multitasking. I'll pick up food to-go from somewhere close by and eat while doing homework in the library. In contrast, my parent's cooking is a form of labor and care that can't be replicated by a dining hall, and being present at the dinner table helped me bond with my family and see what everyone was up to. I miss the flavors of their home-cooked meals and how the food had more variety and healthy options, but I also learned about how important it is to be connected and take time intentionally with people you care about.
